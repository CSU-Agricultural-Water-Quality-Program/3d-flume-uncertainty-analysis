# Figure captions

## Stage-discharge calibration points

Stage-discharge calibration of two 3D-printed flumes and one fiberglass flume; 10 observations per flume. White-filled circles represent posterior medians of observation-specific latent true flow; smaller filled circles represent measured flow and are drawn above the latent estimates. The solid black curve shows the manufacturer equation. Vertical bars show 95% equal-tailed parameter credible intervals for mean rating-curve flow at each observed stage. These bars describe the mean rating curve, rather than uncertainty in the observation-specific latent points; the latent points need not coincide with the centers of these intervals. Horizontal bars show point-gage accuracy bounds of +/- 0.15 cm, without assigning probability coverage. The calibration fits latent stage within these uniform bounds. Vertical intervals evaluate the fitted coefficients at observed stage and are not joint intervals for latent stage and discharge. Panels share axis limits; the reference curve extends from zero to 10% above the maximum observed stage.

## Relative differences from the manufacturer

Percent relative differences between calibration flow and the manufacturer prediction at each observed stage, calculated as 100 x (flow - manufacturer prediction) / manufacturer prediction. White-filled circles represent posterior medians of observation-specific latent true flow; smaller filled circles represent measured flow and are drawn above the latent estimates. The solid black zero line indicates agreement with the manufacturer prediction; negative values indicate lower flow. Vertical bars show 95% equal-tailed parameter credible intervals for the percent relative difference in mean rating-curve flow at each observed stage. These bars describe the mean rating curve, rather than uncertainty in the observation-specific latent points; the latent points need not coincide with the centers of these intervals. Horizontal bars show point-gage accuracy bounds of +/- 0.15 cm, without assigning probability coverage. The calibration fits latent stage within these uniform bounds. Vertical intervals evaluate the fitted coefficients at observed stage and are not joint intervals for latent stage and discharge. Manufacturer predictions are treated as fixed. Relative differences at zero manufacturer flow are undefined and omitted. Panels share axis limits.

## Equation and units

The manufacturer equation is Q (cfs) = 1.55 H (ft)^2.58. Figures report stage in cm and flow in L/s.
